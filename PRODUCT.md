## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, chosen by the user (no build step). GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis vendored in the repo, fonts self-hosted. Must deploy as-is on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel...). Deploy target not specified.

## Users

People living in or around Brest (Harteloire / Saint-Pierre area) looking for a hair salon, often on a phone, often with textured hair (wavy, curly, coily) who have been disappointed elsewhere. Also men and women wanting a precise cut (e.g. wolfcut). Their job: judge in seconds whether this salon will listen and get their hair right, then book.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and booking site for the hair salon "Nuances Brésil Brest Harteloire", 2 Rue Bernard Scheidhauer, 29200 Brest. Success = the visitor books an appointment (primary action, via Planity) or calls (0229615938).

## Positioning

An eco-responsible salon (facade says "Coiffeur éco-responsable"; refill "VRAC" station in the shop) where the team listens, knows how to work textured hair (curly/coily) and teaches clients how to care for it. Client reviews repeat three things: listening, competence on curls, warm welcome.

## Product Principles

1. Listening comes first: the site should feel like the salon sounds in the reviews, attentive and warm, never salesy.
2. Texture is the specialty, not a footnote: curly and coily hair are named plainly.
3. Booking is always one tap away (Planity button and tap-to-call), on every viewport.
4. Eco-responsibility is shown through the refill station and the shop, not asserted with badges.
5. Claims come from supplied truth; anything illustrative is labelled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sufficient contrast, descriptive French alt text, keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured with a lighter version of every animation, simplified heavy motion on mobile.
