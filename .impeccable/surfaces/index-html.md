# Surface brief: index.html (one-page site)

Scope and visitor mode: Persuade. One-page marketing and booking site for Nuances Brésil Brest Harteloire. The visitor must book on Planity or call.

Audience, job, action: people in Brest, often on a phone, often with wavy, curly or coily hair, often let down by other salons. They decide in seconds whether this team will listen and get their hair right. Action: "Prendre rendez-vous" (Planity) or tap-to-call 0229615938, reachable from every viewport.

Proof and content: five verbatim client reviews (no stars, none supplied), five real photos, opening hours, address, Google Maps link. No prices, no client counts, no years of experience: none supplied, none invented. Key figures are limited to 59 h per week, 6 days out of 7, 12 textures (1A to 4C).

Unresolved decisions: domain and canonical URL; real logo file; real service list and prices (Planity holds them); real key figures. Deploy target.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one salon wall, the one in the photos: round mirrors hung on tropical wallpaper under pendant bulbs. Every section is a mirror that opens, by a scroll-driven circle, into the next room, and the visit ends where every haircut ends, at the mirror, where the bulbs light up and the page floods amber. Refuses the category default: cream ground, serif display, terracotta accent, split hero with badge row, rows of equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Grounds sable #EFE7DB, white lin #FBF8F2, galet #D9D0C3, slate ardoise, ink #1F1E1D; the only accent is ampoule amber #F2B233 (light, fills, CTA disc with ink text). Display Bricolage Grotesque heavy and tight, text Figtree. Components are circles: photos in round mirror frames with a thin double ink ring, a disc CTA, discs for controls; cards 14px radius. Tone-on-tone tropical leaf wallpaper drawn as crisp geometric lens leaves with veins; pendant bulbs on cords that sway; a hand-drawn-by-math curl line (prolate cycloid) as secondary thread.

STORY: The visitor understands that this team listens first, knows textured hair, teaches the care, refills instead of wasting. Believes it through the verbatim reviews and the real rooms. Acts: books on Planity or calls, from hero, mobile floating bar, amber CTA and the Nous trouver block.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, giant two-line title "Chaque boucle a sa nuance." in heavy grotesk, one lead sentence, amber disc button "Prendre rendez-vous" plus tap-to-call number, live open/closed pill. Right, a large round mirror (photo 1, internal parallax, double ring) overlapped by a small round mirror (photo 3) and a rotating circular text badge. Three bulbs hang from the top edge and swing toward the cursor. Leaf wallpaper strip on the left edge. Loader: a ring draws, then opens as a circular hole onto this viewport.
